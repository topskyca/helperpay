HelperPay 使用指南

HelperPay 協助你記錄香港 ID 407 外傭的合約、工作日、休息日、法定假日、結算快照及付款。它是參考及記錄工具，並非法律意見或勞工處薪酬產品。

首次設定

  1. 準備 ID 407 合約，輸入簽署日期、兩年合約開始日期、月薪及膳食安排。
  2. 按雙方實際協議選擇休息日是否有薪，以及休息日工作時的補償安排。
  3. 選擇合約採用冬節或聖誕節。程式會載入勞工處2026及2027年法定假日清單。
  4. 只有在同一工資及膳食條款適用於整份兩年合約時，才在設定中確認「整份合約條款」。如中途曾更改，請保留限制並使用官方計算機。

每日記錄

一般工作日不需要逐日輸入。只記錄請假、半日工作、休息日工作或法定假日工作。外傭模式輸入的項目會先標記為待批准；未批准項目不能進入凍結結算單。

休息日工作必須屬自願，並按實際協議記錄現金金額或補回休息日。法定假日工作須提前48小時通知，並安排另定假日；付款不能取代假日。

結算及付款

  1. 在「薪金」查看參考估算及法律檢查清單。
  2. 先處理所有限制。首月／尾月、缺勤扣款、無薪休息日或其他未支援權利會被阻止，並連結至官方計算機。
  3. 所有已支援檢查通過後，按「凍結結算單並記錄付款」。凍結記錄保存輸入資料、計算版本、法律來源版本及校驗碼，日後編輯不會改寫。
  4. 輸入付款方式及截圖，再讓外傭確認收款。舊版本建立而沒有快照的付款會明確標記為舊記錄。

私隱及備份

資料預設只儲存在裝置。匿名統計預設關閉，必須由你在設定中主動啟用。請定期匯出備份並保存在安全位置。詳情請閱讀私隱政策。

需要其他法定權益計算？

年假、疾病津貼、產假／侍產假、終止合約款項、遣散費及長期服務金不由本版本自動計算。請使用勞工處法定僱傭權益參考計算機，並在有爭議或複雜情況下諮詢香港僱傭法律專業人士。

HelperPay User Guide

HelperPay helps record a Hong Kong ID 407 helper’s contract, working days, rest days, statutory holidays, frozen statements and payments. It is a reference and record-keeping tool, not legal advice or a Labour Department payroll product.

First setup

  1. Have the ID 407 contract ready. Enter its signing date, two-year start date, monthly wage and food arrangement.
  2. Select the real agreed terms for rest-day pay and compensation for voluntary rest-day work.
  3. Choose Winter Solstice or Christmas Day. The app loads the Labour Department’s 2026 and 2027 statutory-holiday lists.
  4. Confirm the displayed full-contract terms only if the same wage and food arrangement apply throughout the full two years. If either changed, keep the gate in place and use the official calculator.

Daily records

Normal working days need no daily entry. Record only leave, partial days, rest-day work or statutory-holiday work. Entries made in Helper mode stay pending until the employer approves them; pending items cannot enter a frozen statement.

Rest-day work must be voluntary and use the actually agreed cash amount or substituted rest day. Statutory-holiday work needs 48 hours’ notice and an alternative holiday; cash cannot replace the holiday.

Statements and payments

  1. Open Salary to see a reference estimate and legal-check list.
  2. Resolve every blocker first. Partial first/final months, absence deductions, unpaid rest days and other unsupported entitlements link to the official calculator.
  3. When all supported checks pass, choose Freeze statement & record payment. The frozen record stores inputs, calculation version, legal-source version and checksum; later edits cannot rewrite it.
  4. Add the payment method and screenshot, then ask the helper to acknowledge receipt. Payments created by an older version without a snapshot are clearly marked as legacy records.

Privacy and backups

Data stays on the device by default. Anonymous statistics are off by default and require your opt-in under Settings. Export backups regularly and keep them secure. Read the Privacy Policy.

Other statutory entitlements

This release does not automatically calculate annual leave, sickness allowance, maternity/paternity pay, termination payments, severance or long-service payment. Use the Labour Department Statutory Employment Entitlements Reference Calculator and seek qualified Hong Kong employment-law advice for disputed or complex cases.
